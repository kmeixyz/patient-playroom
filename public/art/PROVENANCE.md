# Original game cover assets

Both assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, one request each, at 1536 × 1024. They were inspected and encoded as WebP at identical dimensions for delivery. No external character or brand assets were used.

## sky.webp
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone original videogame cover art for ages 10–15, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: vivid stylized low-poly 3D mint-green hoverboard on a floating curved sky track, playful lavender stone arches, peach rock islands with saturated lime trees, little golden collectible crystals, blue sky.
Style/medium: polished premium videogame key art, tactile clean geometric surfaces, strong depth and dynamic perspective.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, the hero hoverboard prominent on the right of the frame; a curved elevated track sweeping from foreground into a richly layered distance. Scene fills the entire frame.
Lighting/mood: bright playful sunlit arcade adventure with dimensional soft shadows.
Color palette: turquoise, sky blue, lilac, saturated lime, mint, peach, golden crystal highlights.
Constraints: original scene, no text, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no borders.

## orbit.webp
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone original videogame cover art for a modern teen arcade game, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: a stylized 3D floating friendly spherical robot wearing headphones among colorful orange, cyan and pink planets, orbit hoops and luminous chunky crystals in deep royal blue space.
Style/medium: polished premium videogame key art with tactile vinyl-toy shapes, dimensional smooth surfaces, sophisticated and energetic arcade styling for ages 10–15, no baby aesthetic.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, strong foreground-to-background depth, the headphone robot clearly readable as the main subject, planets and hoops arranged in a dynamic orbital composition. Scene fills the frame.
Lighting/mood: luminous colored edge lighting and playful cosmic adventure.
Color palette: deep royal blue space, vibrant orange, cyan and pink with glowing crystals.
Constraints: original scene, no text, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no borders.
